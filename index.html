About Our Project

We are Team Twenty2. For ECE 5 Lab 4, we are building a line-following robot using an ESP32, photoresistors, DC motors, and PID control. This website documents our work as we finish each challenge.

PID Control

P (Proportional): corrects the robot based on its current error from the line. I (Integral): accounts for accumulated error. D (Derivative): responds to how quickly the error is changing.

We will record the actual Speed, P, I, and D settings we choose for each competition track and explain why we changed them.

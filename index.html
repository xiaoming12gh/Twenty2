Challenge 3B / Controller

How PID controls our line follower

A PID controller uses the difference between the measured line position and its target position to compute a steering correction. The left and right motor speeds are then adjusted to bring the robot back toward the line.

error = measured line position − desired line position
turn rate = kP × error + kI × cumulative error + kD × change in error
P

Proportional

Responds to the robot's current distance from the desired line position.

I

Integral

Accounts for accumulated error to address small persistent offsets.

D

Derivative

Responds to changes in error, helping moderate sudden turns and overshoot.

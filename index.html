4. Explanation of PID Control

P (Proportional) corrects the robot based on the current line-position error. I (Integral) uses accumulated past error to help remove persistent offsets. D (Derivative) responds to how quickly the error changes.

We use the sensor readings to estimate the position of the line, calculate an error, and use PID to adjust the left and right motor speeds. Our final values and tuning explanation for each track will be recorded below.
